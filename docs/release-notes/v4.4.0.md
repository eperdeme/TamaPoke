# Game settings, real age, random starters, and separate hatched & caught Pokédexes

- **New GAME settings page.** Turn to page 3 of Settings to adjust the pace to
  how you play, while keeping all original defaults unless you change them:
  - **Level speed** (`MIN / LV`, 1–60 minutes per level, default 60). Needs,
    egg incubation, passive bond, and the shiny window all stay in real time.
  - **Farewell level** (50–100, default 73), when a companion becomes ready to
    say goodbye.
  - **One-life mode** readout (enabled or disabled for your current save).
  - **Egg new-species preference** (default YES): favour species you have not
    hatched yet when receiving a new egg.
  - All game settings are included in save backups and restores.
- **Real age tracking.** Each creature tracks its real-time age alongside its
  level. The profile card shows real days lived, and the progress card shows
  real minutes remaining to the next level.
- **Random starters.** Pick a random starter on the region or starter selection
  screen if you want to be surprised.
- **Separate Hatched and Caught Pokédexes.** The Pokédex now splits into
  **ALL**, **HATCHED**, **CAUGHT**, and **SHINY** views so completing the
  hatched Pokédex is its own challenge alongside everything you have caught in
  the wild. Existing creatures stay in **ALL**, while **HATCHED** and
  **CAUGHT** track newly hatched and caught creatures going forward.

Your save is kept and upgraded automatically. Before installing, download and
keep a backup, leave **Erase device** unchecked, and keep power connected until
installation finishes. This version passed the emulator tests and firmware
build; it has not been tested on a physical board.
