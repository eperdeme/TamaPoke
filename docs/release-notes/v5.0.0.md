# Jukebox, Step Counter, Three-Tier Pokémon Cries & Visual Overhaul

- **Jukebox & authentic-tempo soundtrack (`MENU → JUKEBOX`).** Collect and play 9 Game Boy tracks (1 unlocked from the start + 8 earned through gym badges, hatching, daily streaks, wild captures, and farewells). Your awake companion dances on screen while music plays and earns **+3 JOY** every 5 minutes (up to 30 minutes per day). Playback stops automatically when you turn off the screen or put your companion to sleep.
- **Step counter & daily walking milestones.** Tracks daily steps in the background using the onboard motion sensor (including a tuned pocket light-sleep cadence) and rewards daily milestones at **500** (+15 Food, +10 Joy), **1,500** (+10 Energy, +10 Joy), **3,000** (+10 Joy, +1 Bond), and **5,000** (+3 Attack/Defence/Speed training points under your IV cap, +20 Joy). Step counting can be toggled **ON / OFF** anytime on **Settings → GAME** without losing today's count.
- **Three-tier Pokémon cries & one-click Web Installer pack.**
  - Install the ready-made **Pokemon Cries** pack directly alongside the region packs on the **Web Installer** (or upload custom `.pak` / `.adp` cry files).
  - Automatically falls back to built-in authentic Gen 1–2 Game Boy cries, or synthesised per-species chirps when no pack is present.
  - Cries now play when hatching, evolving, or switching in a companion, opening registered Pokédex entries, and entering trainer or wild battles.
- **Visual, Settings & audio refresh.** Bold interface typography, solid bezel status arcs, larger raised home action buttons with berry/candy eating animations and updated poop art, upgraded dynamic skies with 7 habitat biomes (including a Graveyard biome for Ghost-type companions), smoother sprite rendering, a unified rim scrollbar across paged pickers and stat cards, and a redesigned 3-page Settings screen with smooth logarithmic volume control.
- **Safer touch controls & battery life.** Removed accidental hold-to-retire on the home screen (retiring remains in `MENU → RETIRE`) and removed unmarked header tap-to-close traps. Improved LiPo battery percentage estimation, low-battery auto-save power-off, and idle screen power saving.

Your save is kept and upgraded automatically. Before installing, download and keep a backup, leave **Erase device** unchecked, and keep power connected until installation finishes.
