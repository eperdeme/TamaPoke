A larger Box, and one less surprising tap when your Party is full.

**Back up before installing, as usual.** Your current creature, Party and all 18 existing Box slots carry over. The update adds 18 empty Box slots without rearranging your collection.

## The Box now holds 36 Pokemon
- Storage doubles from 18 to **36 Pokemon**, across six pages of six.
- Existing Box contents keep their slots. The three new pages begin empty.
- Sorting by slot, number or level still changes only the view, never the saved order.
- Full backups and restores include all 36 Box slots.

## Boxed Pokemon always open their details
- Tapping a boxed Pokemon now always opens its sheet with **TO PARTY** and **RELEASE**, even when all six Party slots are occupied.
- With a full Party, pressing **TO PARTY** then asks which Party member should exchange places with it. Previously the first tap skipped the sheet and jumped straight to that picker, which looked like the Box had simply closed.
- **RELEASE** remains permanent and still requires confirmation.
