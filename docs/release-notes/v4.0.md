Saves no longer lose badges after a restart, and battle music no longer keeps playing once the fight is over.

**You do not need to do anything before installing.** Your creature, party, box, badges, Pokedex and streak all carry over. Back up anyway from the installer page — that button exists for a reason.

## Badges, and sometimes the whole save, could reset to zero
- Playing normally could quietly fill up the board's save storage, and the very next restart after that erased everything: every badge, the whole Pokedex, and the save itself, back to a blank slate. This is what issue #5 reported.
- Saving is far lighter now, so this should no longer happen in normal play.
- If the board's storage is ever close to full, a small warning triangle now appears next to the battery icon so you can back up before restarting, rather than finding out by being wiped.

## Battle music no longer keeps playing after the fight
- Losing a wild battle, or losing to a trainer, used to leave the battle music playing under whatever screen you opened next.
- A battle now always stops its music when it ends, whichever way it ends.

## One thing to know
- This update changes how your save is stored on the board. It keeps carrying forward through this and every future update, same as always. Going back to an OLDER firmware afterward is not supported — there is no reason to, but if you ever do, back up first.

## What to do
- Just install this firmware. Nothing else is required.
