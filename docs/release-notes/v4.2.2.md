# More reliable encounters, battles, and bond

- Evolved wild Pokemon now appear only when their full evolution line's level
  requirements are met. Azumarill and Rhyperior, for example, no longer appear
  before levels 25 and 42 respectively.
- Battle move choices are more complete, and fixed-damage attacks now count
  toward type coverage. Druddigon keeps its Dragon Rage when its moves are set.
- The active creature gains one bond point per hour when all four needs are at
  least 50. Passive gains are limited to 10 points per day across all creatures.
  A creature already at maximum bond no longer uses another creature's share.

No save reset or special migration is needed. Before installing, keep a backup,
leave **Erase device** unchecked, and keep power connected until installation
finishes. This version passed the emulator tests and firmware build; it has not
been tested on a physical board.
