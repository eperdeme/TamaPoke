# More room, with safer collection saves

- Store up to 1,200 Pokemon in the Box, plus six in your party and one active
  companion: 1,207 in total.
- Your existing party and Box are copied into dedicated internal-flash storage.
  Two copies are checked before the new collection is activated, and the old
  collection is left untouched during migration.
- Collection saves no longer compete with your companion's save for the old,
  small storage area. Failed moves and releases are reported instead of
  quietly losing a creature.
- Backups include the expanded collection. Older backups can still be restored;
  interrupted restores resume before normal play.
- The new save format has room for larger future collection records. More flash
  space is reserved for future companion-save improvements, without moving the
  companion or player save again in this update.

Before upgrading, download a verified save backup and keep it somewhere safe.
Leave "Erase device" unchecked and keep power connected until the update and
migration finish.

Do not install older firmware afterwards: it cannot read the expanded collection
or the new backups. The red save-warning triangle still reports real failures;
it has not been disabled.

Hardware upgrade, power-interruption and soak testing are required before this
build is distributed to customers.
