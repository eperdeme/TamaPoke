# Type quiz, a medals screen, and fairer farewells

- New **MEDALS** screen, between your trainer card and your party. It opens on
  today's checklist; turn the rim to see the medals your creature has earned.
  Your trainer card now shows only your profile and badges.
- The colour memory game is now a **type quiz**: choose the type that is super
  effective against the one shown, before time runs out. Your best memory-game
  score is kept separately, so your new quiz record starts from zero.
- A creature says farewell only once. If you make it active again from the
  party, it will not be offered a second farewell.
- Bond from petting and care actions now has one shared daily limit across all
  your creatures. Swapping creatures or restarting does not reset that limit.
- **Choose your evolution.** Creatures with more than one possible evolution
  (Eevee, Wurmple, Kirlia, Poliwhirl, Gloom, Slowpoke, Tyrogue and others) now
  let you pick which form to become, instead of it being chosen at random.
  Only forms whose region pack is installed are offered. Swipe down to decide
  later.
- Bulbasaur, Charmander and Squirtle are now rare in Kanto eggs and
  encounters. An egg already waiting is rerolled only if its rarity changed.

Your save is kept and upgraded automatically. Before installing, keep a backup,
leave **Erase device** unchecked, and keep power connected until installation
finishes. This version passed the emulator tests and firmware build; it has not
been tested on a physical board.
