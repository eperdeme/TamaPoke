# More room for your Pokemon

- The Box now holds 1,200 Pokemon, in addition to six party members and your
  active companion.
- Your existing party and Box move into dedicated storage. Two expanded copies
  are checked before the old collection copies are removed. This also frees
  space for your companion and player settings, addressing the red triangle
  caused by duplicate old copies filling the smaller save area.
- If both expanded copies cannot be verified, the old copies are kept. The red
  triangle still warns about save failures or low space; it has not been hidden.
- Backups include the expanded collection, and older backups can still be
  restored. Interrupted restores resume before normal play.

Before upgrading, download a verified backup and keep it somewhere safe. Leave
**Erase device** unchecked, and keep power connected until the update and
migration finish.

Do not install older firmware afterwards: it cannot read the expanded
collection or the new backups. Power-interruption and long-running hardware
tests are still pending.
