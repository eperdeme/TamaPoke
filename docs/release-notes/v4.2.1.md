# More room for reliable saves

- After the expanded collection is verified in two copies, the device removes
  its obsolete extra copies from the smaller save area. Your Pokemon stay in
  the expanded collection; this gives the active companion and player settings
  more room to save.
- If both expanded copies cannot be verified, the old copies are kept and the
  save warning remains visible. The warning has not been hidden or weakened.

Keep a verified backup before installing, and leave **Erase device** unchecked.
